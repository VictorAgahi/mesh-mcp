- Add `trace_flow`, the eighth MCP tool (9.0 T3): end-to-end event chains hop by hop. Entries: an event / topic, an HTTP route (`VERB /path`), a handler or a scheduled method (`Class.method`; `entry_kind: cron` is accepted, schedules arrive with T4). `direction: forward` (entry -> resolved calls -> publish -> next consumers) or `reverse` (publish <- callers <- entry <- upstream producers). Every step prints a `path:line`: a call step gives the callee declaration and the call-site line, with the resolution rule; per-step confidence, hop and chain confidence (weakest). Bounds: `event_depth` (default 3, max 5), `call_depth` (default 6, max 12), `fan_out` (default 20, max 50, rest reported as `+K more`), 2,000 hops per trace; paged by hop with `offset` under the 48 KB cap. Iterative walks with visited sets: cyclic event chains and cyclic call graphs end and say "already traced".
- New chain engine `contracts::flow` (`ContractGraph::trace_flow`), generic over `Calls` edges: Java/Spring, Go and Python chains follow once their call extraction lands, with no change to the engine.
- `analyze_impact` with `depth > 1` now runs on the same engine: past the first hop it follows resolved calls from each consumer to the publishes they reach (a layered consumer -> service -> publisher in three files was a "Chain end" in 8.x). Where no call path reaches a publish, the 8.x same-file rule is the fallback, still labelled `heuristic`. `depth: N` lists the producers, topics and consumers of `trace_flow` with `event_depth: N - 1`; a shared test checks the agreement on the fixtures. `depth: 1` output is unchanged.
- `scripts/golden/score_events.py --chain-tool trace_flow` passes `event_depth` and the golden entry kind, and follows the `More hops` footer. Chains on the golden corpora (strict order, rebased on T2b): `analyze_impact` depth 5 2/30 (8.0.1: 2/30), `trace_flow` 17/30 (layered-ts 10/10, layered-go 4/4, polyglot-shop 2/2, unresolved-flow 1/2; layered-py 0/2 (1 of 4 hops resolved), spring-monolith 0/5 and nestjs-monolith-bus 0/5 (in-process buses, T4)).
- Go entry and producer sites on a `NewT` constructor line are anchored on the methods of `T` that use its fields (the rule the calls derive already applied): `ContractGraph::site_callables`.
- No parse cache change (`SCHEMA_VERSION` unchanged).
