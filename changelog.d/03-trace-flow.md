- Add `trace_flow`, the eighth MCP tool (9.0 T3): end-to-end event chains hop by hop. Entries: an event / topic, an HTTP route (`VERB /path`), a handler or a scheduled method (`Class.method`; `entry_kind: cron` is accepted, schedules arrive with T4). `direction: forward` (entry -> resolved calls -> publish -> next consumers) or `reverse` (publish <- callers <- entry <- upstream producers). Every step prints a `path:line`: a call step gives the callee declaration and the call-site line, with the resolution rule; per-step confidence, hop and chain confidence (weakest). Bounds in the schema with minimum and maximum: `event_depth` (default 3, 1-5), `call_depth` (default 6, 1-12), `fan_out` (default 20, 1-50: callees, publishes per entry, consumers / producers per topic, entry sites; rest reported as `+K more`); larger values are clamped and shown, invalid ones refused with a message naming the argument. At most 2,000 hops per trace, enforced per hop (**Hop limit reached**; topics left unexpanded say "not traced: the hop limit"). Paged by hop with `offset` under the 48 KB cap. Iterative walks with visited sets: cyclic event chains and cyclic call graphs end and say "already traced". A walk cut by `call_depth` says **Call depth limit reached** and does not fall back on the same-file rule.
- New chain engine `contracts::flow` (`ContractGraph::trace_flow`), generic over `Calls` edges: TypeScript (T2a), Java / Spring, Go and Python (T2b) all go through it. Go consumer / producer sites bound in a `NewT` constructor are anchored on the methods of `T` that call through a field (`ContractGraph::site_callables`, recorded by the calls derive); that anchoring is an inference, so a step reached through it, and its chain, are at most `heuristic` (layered-go: the consume steps and hop 1.1 `BillingConsumer.Run` are now `heuristic`, was `exact`; the chains stay `ambiguous`, their weakest step being the `Publisher` interface dispatch).
- `analyze_impact` with `depth > 1` now runs on the same engine: past the first hop it follows resolved calls from each consumer to the publishes they reach (a layered consumer -> service -> publisher in three files was a "Chain end" in 8.x). Where no call path reaches a publish, the 8.x same-file rule is the fallback, still labelled `heuristic`. `depth: N` lists the producers, topics and consumers of `trace_flow` with `event_depth: N - 1`; a shared test checks the agreement on the fixtures.
- `analyze_impact` `depth: 1` output is unchanged by this PR: identical to the parent p9/base 90a38b8 on all 58 golden targets (every topic and chain entry of the 7 corpora). It is NOT identical to 8.0.1: 23 of those 58 outputs differ, all in producer rows, because of T2a / T2b (already in the base), which anchor producers on the publish call instead of the enclosing handler (e.g. unresolved-flow `chain:1`: 8.0.1 "producer `listenChain0` `src/chain0.ts:5`", now "producer `chain:1` `src/chain0.ts:7`, via `listenChain0`").
- `scripts/golden/score_events.py --chain-tool trace_flow` passes `event_depth` and the golden entry kind, and follows the `More hops` footer. New like-for-like slice: chains with a topic entry and forward direction (the only ones `analyze_impact` accepts), with "endpoints present" (every consume / produce step in the answer, any order). Numbers (golden corpora, 30 chains):
  - all chains, strict order: `trace_flow` 17/30; `analyze_impact` depth 5 2/30 (8.0.1 2/30). Not like-for-like: `trace_flow` prints call steps and receives the entry kind and direction; `analyze_impact` cannot list call steps and is given route, handler and cron entries as topic strings.
